Keys and tips

V select · H or middle-drag pan · wheel zoom · F fit view · Space run/pause · Del remove · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo/redo · [ ] brush size

Drag an object to move it. Drag the square on its ring to resize. Drag the round handle on an arrow to aim it.

Push and Calm zones can be rectangles: pick Rectangle in the shape box above the map and drag along the water. Painting has a Rectangle mode too.

Every slider has a box next to it. Type a value and press Enter. Sizes and strengths can go past the end of the slider.

Check alignment with "$flow_debug" "1" in the water material. The flowmap colours then show on the water in Hammer and in game.

If water runs the wrong way in game, tick Flip red and/or Flip green in Export and export again.
